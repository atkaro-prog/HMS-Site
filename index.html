Questions

Frequently asked.

Who is behind Hamptons Motorized Shades?

We’re part of Home Technology Experts, a Southampton-based integrator serving the East End and Manhattan. Your shades are designed, installed, programmed and supported by the same in-house team that handles HTE’s lighting, audio and smart-home projects.

Can motorized shades be added to an existing home?

Yes. Battery-powered and plug-in motors make retrofits clean and simple, with no need to open walls. For renovations and new construction we coordinate hardwired solutions with your electrician.

How long do battery motors last?

Most modern battery systems run one to three years between charges or battery changes, depending on how often the shades move.

Will they work with my smart-home system?

We integrate with leading platforms including Lutron, Control4, Savant, Apple Home and Alexa, as well as simple wall keypads and handheld remotes.

How long does a project take?

Most projects move from consultation to installation in a matter of weeks, depending on fabric selection and scope. We’re happy to plan around your season and arrival dates.

Do you work with designers and builders?

Absolutely. We partner with architects, interior designers and builders from early planning through final programming to make sure pockets, power and fabrics are right the first time.
